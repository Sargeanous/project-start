# UAE Media Content Standards

- **Doc ID:** MCS
- **Source:** UAE Media Content Standards (as published by the UAE Media Council / formerly National Media Council). Text below follows the official English publication of the standards.
- **Jurisdiction:** United Arab Emirates (federal)
- **Applies to:** All media content, including advertising displayed on DOOH / outdoor screens
- **Status:** Authoritative basis for content screening. Verify against the latest official publication before legal reliance.
- **Tags:** content-standards, religion, national-identity, public-morals, children, misinformation

Each clause has a stable ID. Cite clauses as `MCS-NN`.

---

## Clauses

**MCS-01 — Respect for religion.** Respect the Divine Entity, Islamic beliefs, monotheistic religions, and other beliefs; content must not offend any of them.

**MCS-02 — Respect for the State.** Respect the State's governance system, its symbols and institutions, and the highest interests of the State and society.

**MCS-03 — State policy.** Respect the State's orientation and policy at the internal and international levels.

**MCS-04 — Foreign relations.** Avoid addressing anything that might harm the State's foreign relations.

**MCS-05 — Heritage and identity.** Respect the cultural and civilisational heritage and national identity of the UAE.

**MCS-06 — National unity.** Do not publish or circulate anything that harms national unity and social cohesion.

**MCS-07 — Strife and incitement.** Do not raise sectarian, regional, or tribal strife; do not incite violence, hatred, or terrorist acts; do not incite hatred or spread a spirit of discord in society.

**MCS-08 — Prevailing values.** Do not offend the prevailing values in society; observe the requirements of the public interest.

**MCS-09 — Legal and economic systems.** Do not offend the legal, economic, judicial, and security system in the UAE.

**MCS-10 — Privacy.** Respect the rules of privacy and everything related to the private lives of individuals.

**MCS-11 — Crime incitement.** Do not publish or circulate anything that might incite the commission of crimes or encourage murder, rape, or the use of drugs or psychotropic substances.

**MCS-12 — Public morals.** Do not publish, broadcast, or circulate phrases, pictures, drawings, or opinions that violate public morals, offend young people, or call for embracing or promoting subversive ideas.

**MCS-13 — False information.** Do not publish, broadcast, or circulate false news, forged papers, or materials incorrectly attributed to entities or persons.

**MCS-14 — Economy and currency.** Do not publish, broadcast, or circulate anything that might harm the national currency or the economic situation in the State.

**MCS-15 — Rumours.** Do not spread rumours and misleading news.

**MCS-16 — Destructive groups.** Do not glorify groups with destructive political, ethnic, economic, ideological, or social tendencies that are adverse to the UAE or that serve their own interests.

**MCS-17 — Contributors.** Select appropriate individuals when hosting or accepting contributions in the media.

**MCS-18 — Advertising alignment.** The content of an advertisement shall respect the culture, identity, and values of the UAE.

**MCS-19 — Age rating.** Take into account the Age Rating System of artistic works approved by the national media authority.

**MCS-20 — Children's rights.** Respect children's rights in accordance with the legislation applicable within the UAE.
