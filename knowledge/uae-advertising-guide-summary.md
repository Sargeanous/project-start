# UAE Advertising Guide — Working Summary

- **Doc ID:** ADG
- **Source:** Internal working summary of the UAE Advertisement Guide (National Media Council / UAE Media Council advertising standards, incl. Cabinet Resolution No. 41 of 2018 concerning the Advertisement Guide) and related sector rules.
- **Jurisdiction:** United Arab Emirates (federal); emirate-level permit rules noted where relevant
- **Applies to:** Commercial advertising on DOOH / outdoor screens
- **Status:** WORKING SUMMARY drafted for screening use. The official scanned guide (PDF) is the source of truth; verify clauses against it before legal reliance.
- **Tags:** advertising, prohibited-categories, restricted-categories, truthfulness, children, health, financial, real-estate, promotions, arabic

Each clause has a stable ID. Cite clauses as `ADG-N.N`.

## 1. General principles

**ADG-1.1 — Legality.** Advertisements must comply with UAE laws and regulations and with the Media Content Standards (see MCS).

**ADG-1.2 — Truthfulness.** Advertisements must be honest and truthful. Misleading, deceptive, or exaggerated claims that cannot be substantiated are prohibited (see also MCS-13, MCS-15).

**ADG-1.3 — Cultural respect.** Advertisements must respect the culture, identity, religious values, and social norms of the UAE (see MCS-01, MCS-05, MCS-18).

**ADG-1.4 — Decency.** Images and language must observe public morals: no nudity, sexually suggestive content, or indecent gestures/wording (see MCS-12).

**ADG-1.5 — No disparagement.** Comparative advertising must not disparage competitors, their products, or their trademarks.

**ADG-1.6 — National symbols.** Use of the UAE flag, national emblem, images of the leadership, currency imagery, or government logos in advertising is restricted and requires approval from the competent authority.

**ADG-1.7 — Advertiser identification.** Advertising must be recognisable as advertising and must identify the advertiser where relevant; content must not be incorrectly attributed (see MCS-13).

## 2. Prohibited categories (do not advertise)

**ADG-2.1 — Alcohol.** Advertising of alcoholic beverages is prohibited in public media and outdoor advertising.

**ADG-2.2 — Narcotics.** Advertising of narcotics or psychotropic substances is prohibited (see MCS-11).

**ADG-2.3 — Gambling.** Advertising of gambling and games of chance is prohibited except where specifically licensed by the competent federal authority; treat as prohibited by default and escalate any licensed-operator claim for verification.

**ADG-2.4 — Tobacco.** Advertising of tobacco products, e-cigarettes, and vaping products is prohibited.

**ADG-2.5 — Indecent services.** Advertising of prostitution, escort services, or any sexually oriented services is prohibited.

**ADG-2.6 — Sorcery.** Advertising of sorcery, witchcraft, fortune-telling, or similar services is prohibited.

**ADG-2.7 — Counterfeit goods.** Advertising of counterfeit or infringing products is prohibited.

**ADG-2.8 — Weapons.** Advertising of weapons, ammunition, or explosives to the general public is prohibited without authorisation from the competent authority.

**ADG-2.9 — Hate and extremism.** Any advertising containing or alluding to hate speech, extremist symbols, or content inciting violence is prohibited (see MCS-07).

## 3. Restricted categories (permit / pre-approval required)

**ADG-3.1 — Health and medical.** Advertisements for medicines, medical devices, clinics, treatments, and health claims require prior approval from the UAE health authority (MOHAP or the emirate health authority). Cure promises and unverifiable health claims are prohibited.

**ADG-3.2 — Financial products.** Advertisements for investments, securities, crypto assets, and credit must comply with Central Bank / SCA rules; guaranteed-return or misleading profit claims are prohibited.

**ADG-3.3 — Real estate.** Real-estate advertising requires the relevant emirate permit (e.g. DLD/RERA in Dubai; DMT in Abu Dhabi) and must display the required permit/project registration numbers.

**ADG-3.4 — Food claims.** Nutrition and health claims on food must be accurate; halal claims must be certified.

**ADG-3.5 — Promotions and offers.** Promotions, discounts, and prize draws require the applicable economic-department permit; material terms (dates, conditions, limits) must be stated clearly, with no hidden conditions.

**ADG-3.6 — Education and recruitment.** Advertising educational qualifications or overseas recruitment requires the relevant authority approvals; misleading qualification or job claims are prohibited.

**ADG-3.7 — Charity fundraising.** Fundraising advertisements require a licence from the competent authority; unlicensed donation appeals are prohibited.

## 4. Children

**ADG-4.1 — No exploitation.** Advertising must not exploit children's inexperience or credulity, and must not directly urge children to purchase or to pressure others to purchase (see MCS-20).

**ADG-4.2 — Safety.** Advertising must not show children in unsafe situations or encourage dangerous behaviour.

**ADG-4.3 — Age-appropriate content.** Content near schools or child-oriented contexts must be appropriate for general audiences, taking the Age Rating System into account (see MCS-19).

## 5. Language

**ADG-5.1 — Arabic accuracy.** Where Arabic copy is used, it must be grammatically correct and faithful to the meaning of any paired English copy.

**ADG-5.2 — Bilingual parity.** For public-facing and government-adjacent placements, Arabic-first or Arabic-parallel copy is expected; the Arabic and English versions must convey the same claims. Discrepant claims across languages are treated as misleading (see ADG-1.2).

## 6. Outdoor / DOOH placement rules

**ADG-6.1 — Traffic safety.** Outdoor creative must not mimic traffic signs or signals, use flashing/strobing effects that could distract drivers, or otherwise compromise road safety. Emirate authority rules (e.g. ITC / DMT in Abu Dhabi, RTA in Dubai) apply to placement, brightness, and motion.

**ADG-6.2 — Legibility.** Creative must remain legible and unambiguous at viewing distance; critical terms of an offer may not be presented in illegibly small text.

**ADG-6.3 — Context sensitivity.** Creative near mosques, schools, hospitals, or memorial contexts must be appropriate to that context; scheduling should respect prayer times and national observances where directed by the authority.
